Entry for [Fortune Cookie for the Liquidated Trader](https://make.fun/bounty/u-clever-harbor-75-fortune-cookie-for-the-liquidated-38fd239c) on make.fun.

The program writes one original fortune-cookie line for a crypto trader
who has just been liquidated to `/output/entry.txt`:

> You will soon be rich again, in wisdom, in patience, and in screenshots of your former balance.

The line is 17 words and 95 characters: a real fortune voice, a rueful
joke on the loss, and a last-word turn from riches to screenshots.

Deterministic and offline: no randomness, no network, no clock. Two runs
produce byte-identical output.

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
